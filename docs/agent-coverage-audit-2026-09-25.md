# Agent coverage and Mock-mode check — September 25, 2026

The Agent's permission-gated workspace reader currently covers Team, Meetings, Offerings, FDL Components, Market Intel, Leads, Opportunities, Solutioning, Contracts, Customers, Goals, and Reports. Answers about these records still depend on the user's module permissions and the source data present in the selected workspace. The earlier [50-question audit](agent-50-query-audit.md) covers representative questions, links, and permission boundaries in more depth.

The route inventory also includes Dashboard, Pipeline, Forecast, Analytics, Activity, Contacts, Sessions, Tasks, Campaigns, Sequences, Voice, Revenue Accruals, and several administration and planning pages. These are **not all independent Agent read modules**. Some information is reachable indirectly through the covered records, but the Agent cannot yet promise the same depth, calculations, or actions as every page. In particular, this pass did not verify page parity for those routes or enable CRM writes through the Agent. Headless CRM use therefore remains a product goal, not a completed capability.

Five paid Mock-mode questions checked goal names and monthly values, annual versus monthly targets, conversation handoff, and an Opportunities-plus-Solutioning answer. One test caught the model substituting Marketing Qualified Leads for the exact name “Marketing campaigns”; after the grounding change it correctly said the exact goal was absent in this Mock workspace. A Renewals follow-up returned August verified value of $3,799,268, zero pending/sent-back, and no monthly target, matching the underlying goal data. A cross-page question returned linked opportunities, owners, sign dates, original currencies, and linked or missing solutioning requests. The floating chat stayed open after page interaction, and opening full chat retained the tested conversation.

Limits: these checks do not establish that every page and every workspace datum is available to the Agent; a broad data-access claim would be false. The cross-page answer was inspected for rendering and links, but every returned record was not independently reconciled with its source page. The one-time retry reduces transient connection failures; it cannot guarantee external model availability. No load test, deployment, or production validation was performed. Further work should add permission-scoped readers and answer checks for the remaining high-use routes, starting with Pipeline, Forecast, Revenue Accruals, Sessions, Tasks, and Campaigns, then verify parity against their source pages with bounded questions.
