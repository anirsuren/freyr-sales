Q: What is this page for and how do I use it?
A: This is Freyr Sales Intelligence, our internal sales platform. The left rail is your whole toolkit: Agent (that's me, for questions like this one), Offerings (our product catalogue), FDL Components, Customers, Team, Reports, Performance, Market Intel (live news, posts and signals on tracked companies), and Admin.

To use it, just search or browse from the top bar, which jumps you straight to offerings, companies or pages, or click into any left-rail item for that specific view. Each page also has its own local search for the list on it, and you can flip between Real (your live workspace) and Mock (practice data) from the account menu, top right, if you want to try things without touching real records.

What are you trying to get done, I can point you to the right spot.

PATH: /components
Q: How do I mark a component version as released?
A: To mark a component version as released, go to the **FDL Components** page in the left rail. Find the version on the timeline and set its status to "released," along with the date for it. That's the same place you'd mark a version as "expected" or "current" too, since all three statuses live on that per-version timeline view.

PATH: /customers
Q: How do I change who owns an account?
A: You can do that from the **Customers** page: select the account row (or rows) you want to change, then use the bulk action to reassign the owner. That's also where you'd export a CSV if you needed the list. Since it's a bulk action, if you're changing just one account, you can select just that single row before applying it.


========================================
PATH: /customers
Q: What does the Components tab on a customer show me?
A: Opening a customer record gives you tabs for its components, activity, offerings, and interaction history, so the Components tab is where you'd see the Freyr product components tied to that account, following the same version timeline (expected, current, released) that FDL Components tracks platform-wide. It's essentially a customer-scoped view of which components and versions that account is on. If you want the full timeline mechanics, that's covered on the FDL Components page in the left rail.

PATH: /team
Q: How do I message someone on this page?
A: You can message a teammate right from the Team page: each rep's card has a Microsoft Teams chat link next to their photo and details, just click it and it opens a Teams chat with them. That's for internal colleagues only, client contacts show a phone number instead since Teams won't apply there.


========================================
PATH: /reports
Q: How do I get this data out into Excel?
A: You've got a few options depending on what you need. On the **Reports** page, hit **Export CSV** to get the whole revenue book, headline totals, revenue per offering, and every renewal date. If you're after goals and logged entries specifically, **Performance** has its own export button. And on **Customers**, you can select the rows you want and export just those. All of them come out as CSV, so they open straight in Excel.
